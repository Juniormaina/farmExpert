# Facilitator guide

Read this before the first farmer opens the site. The pilot is web only, maize, Nakuru, with you in the room. SMS and USSD are not part of the pilot.

## Say this first

Farm Expert can:

- help calculate a planning budget
- compare demonstration fertilizer prices
- show demonstration market figures
- help structure a farming question

Farm Expert cannot:

- guarantee profit
- guarantee yield
- diagnose a crop disease from text
- prescribe a pesticide dose
- provide a verified live market price in the current demo configuration

Then say, in the farmer's language:

> The market and fertilizer figures are demonstration data, not today's verified market quotations.

## Step in when

- The farmer asks for a pesticide treatment. Tell them to ask an extension officer. Do not treat the app's refusal as a diagnosis.
- The farmer asks what disease the plant has. The app will refuse. You should say the same thing.
- The farmer reads a demo price as today's price. Point at the demo notice.
- The farmer treats an illustrative fertilizer rate as an approved recommendation. The rates in this build are not signed off. See `docs/AGRONOMIC_REVIEW.md`.

## Help

Open the menu and Help. The support line must show the real contact. If it says the contact is not configured, stop the session and tell the technical owner.

Delete farm details removes the profile from that phone only. It does not delete another farmer's feedback, because feedback is not tied to a name.
